# Appendix: Initial Seed Prompts (N=23)

These are the 23 starting prompts used for the recursive inference runs. Each prompt was fed into GPT-5 Standard to generate Output t=0, which then served as Input t=1.

### Categories Overview
The runs were divided into 5 distinct semantic classes to measure drift across different domains:

#### **1. Factual (5 runs)**
* "Explain the second law of thermodynamics and its relationship to entropy."
* "Describe the process of photosynthesis in C3 plants."
* "Summarize the key events of the Industrial Revolution."
* "What is the difference between DNA and RNA?"
* "Explain the mechanism of plate tectonics."

#### **2. Logic (5 runs)**
* "Explain the difference between inductive and deductive reasoning."
* "Analyze the validity of the following syllogism: All humans are mortal; Socrates is human; therefore, Socrates is mortal."
* "If a person is in Paris, they are in France. Pierre is not in France. Is Pierre in Paris? Explain your logic."
* "Compare the P vs NP problem in computer science to a real-world puzzle."
* "Describe the 'Ship of Theseus' paradox and its implications for identity."

#### **3. Creative (5 runs)**
* "Write a short story about a lighthouse keeper at the edge of the universe."
* "Describe a color that doesn't exist to someone who cannot see."
* "Compose a dialogue between a human and an ancient, sentient tree."
* "Imagine a society where music is the primary currency. How does it work?"
* "Write a poem about the silence of a high-performance computer server room."

#### **4. Code (4 runs)**
* "Write a Python script to generate all prime numbers up to 1000 using the Sieve of Erathosthenes."
* "Explain the concept of recursion in programming with a simple, functional example."
* "Write a function to calculate the Euclidean distance between two points in 3D space."
* "What is the structural and performance difference between a list and a tuple in Python?"

#### **5. Abstract (4 runs)**
* "Discuss the concept of 'being' in Heideggerian philosophy."
* "Define beauty from a non-anthropocentric perspective."
* "Analyze the relationship between human language and objective reality."
* "What does it mean for a mathematical system to be 'stable'?"
